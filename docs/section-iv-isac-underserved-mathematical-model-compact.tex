\section{ISAC-Assisted Underserved-Region Model}
\label{sec:mathematical-model}

Let $\mathcal{U}$ denote the ground UEs and $\mathcal{Q}$ the UAV
gNBs. In the simulation, every UAV gNB carries a co-located
transmit--receive ISAC subsystem and acts as a sensing node; hence,
$\mathcal{S}=\mathcal{Q}$. Terrestrial gNBs and terrestrial sensing
devices do not perform radar sensing. The ground UEs, or their
associated monitored targets, are passive targets with radar cross
section (RCS) $\sigma_u$. Communication measurements identify weakly
served UEs, while ISAC observations estimate their horizontal
locations for UAV repositioning.

\subsection{System-Level ISAC Sensing}

Under a monostatic system-level sensing abstraction, the 3-D range
and received echo power between UAV $s$ and UE $u$ are
\begin{align}
 r_{s,u}(t)&=\|\mathbf{p}_s(t)-\mathbf{p}_u(t)\|_2,\label{eq:isac-range}\\
 P^{\rm r}_{s,u}(t)&=
 \frac{P_sG_s^2\lambda^2\sigma_u}
 {(4\pi)^3r_{s,u}^4(t)L_s}.\label{eq:isac-echo}
\end{align}
Here, $P_s$, $G_s$, $\lambda$, and $L_s$ are the sensing power,
antenna gain, wavelength, and aggregate system loss. With noise
density $N_0$, bandwidth $B_s$, and noise factor $F_s$, the echo SNR
is
\begin{equation}
 \gamma_{s,u}(t)=
 \frac{P^{\rm r}_{s,u}(t)}{N_0B_sF_s}.
 \label{eq:isac-snr-compact}
\end{equation}

The simulator uses the following probabilistic detection abstraction:
\begin{align}
 p^{\rm D}_{s,u}(t)&=
 \left[1+e^{-a(\gamma^{\rm dB}_{s,u}(t)-\gamma_0)}\right]^{-1},\\
 d_{s,u}(t)&\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}
 \bigl(p^{\rm D}_{s,u}(t)\bigr).
 \label{eq:isac-detection-compact}
\end{align}
Here, $a$ and $\gamma_0$ control the slope and midpoint. For a
successful detection, the horizontal position estimate is
\begin{align}
 \widehat{\mathbf{p}}^{xy}_{s,u}(t)
 &=\mathbf{p}^{xy}_u(t)+\mathbf{e}_{s,u}(t),\label{eq:isac-position}\\
 \mathbf{e}_{s,u}(t)&\sim\mathcal{N}
 \bigl(\mathbf{0},\sigma_{s,u}^2(t)\mathbf{I}_2\bigr).
\end{align}
This localisation-error model is a system-level simulation
abstraction. Defining
\begin{equation}
 \Delta\gamma_{s,u}(t)=
 \gamma^{\rm dB}_{s,u}(t)-\gamma_{\rm ref},
\end{equation}
its standard deviation is
\begin{equation}
 \sigma_{s,u}(t)=\operatorname{clip}
 \left(\sigma_{\rm ref}10^{-\Delta\gamma_{s,u}(t)/20},
 \sigma_{\min},\sigma_{\max}\right).
 \label{eq:isac-error-compact}
\end{equation}

Let $\mathcal{S}_u(t)=\{s\in\mathcal{S}:d_{s,u}(t)=1\}$ and
$w_{s,u}(t)=\sigma_{s,u}^{-2}(t)$. Assuming independent, unbiased
Gaussian localisation errors, the near-RT RIC forms
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{\mathbf{p}}^{xy}_u(t)=
 \frac{\sum_{s\in\mathcal{S}_u(t)}w_{s,u}(t)
 \widehat{\mathbf{p}}^{xy}_{s,u}(t)}
 {\sum_{s\in\mathcal{S}_u(t)}w_{s,u}(t)}.
 \label{eq:isac-fusion-compact}
\end{equation}
No estimate is produced when $\mathcal{S}_u(t)=\varnothing$. True UE
coordinates are used only inside the sensing-measurement generator
and for offline evaluation; the controller receives only fused
estimates.

\subsection{Underserved UEs and UAV Repositioning}

The implemented controller uses serving RSRP $R_u(t)$ to define
\begin{equation}
 I_u(t)=\mathbf{1}\{R_u(t)<R_{\min}\}.
 \label{eq:underserved-compact}
\end{equation}
Thus, the UEs available to the clustering controller are
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{\mathcal{U}}_{\rm US}(t)=
 \{u\in\mathcal{U}:I_u(t)=1,\,
 \mathcal{S}_u(t)\neq\varnothing\}.
 \label{eq:sensed-underserved-set}
\end{equation}
Let $\mathcal{Q}_{\rm a}(t)$ contain UAVs with remaining serving
capacity. The number of clusters is
\begin{equation}
 K(t)=\min\bigl\{|\mathcal{Q}_{\rm a}(t)|,
 |\widehat{\mathcal{U}}_{\rm US}(t)|\bigr\}.
 \label{eq:k-selection}
\end{equation}
Ordinary K-means determines the horizontal target centroids:
\begin{equation}
 \{\boldsymbol{\mu}_k^\star\}=
 \arg\min_{\{\boldsymbol{\mu}_k\}}
 \sum_{u\in\widehat{\mathcal{U}}_{\rm US}(t)}
 \min_{1\leq k\leq K}
 \|\widehat{\mathbf{p}}^{xy}_u(t)-\boldsymbol{\mu}_k\|_2^2.
 \label{eq:kmeans-compact}
\end{equation}
Each available UAV is greedily assigned once to its nearest
unassigned centroid, matching the implemented controller. For UAV
$j$ and assigned centroid $\boldsymbol{\mu}_{k(j)}^\star$, define
\begin{align}
 \boldsymbol{\delta}_j(t)&=
 \boldsymbol{\mu}_{k(j)}^\star-\mathbf{q}^{xy}_j(t),\\
 \alpha_j(t)&=\min\left\{1,
 \frac{v_{\max}\Delta t}{\|\boldsymbol{\delta}_j(t)\|_2}\right\}.
\end{align}
The horizontal update is
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf{q}^{xy}_j(t+\Delta t)=
 \mathbf{q}^{xy}_j(t)+\alpha_j(t)\boldsymbol{\delta}_j(t).
 \label{eq:movement-compact}
\end{equation}
When $\|\boldsymbol{\delta}_j(t)\|_2=0$, displacement is zero;
altitude remains fixed and flight-area constraints are enforced.

We evaluate detection probability, localisation RMSE, and matched
centroid error, followed by the underserved-UE fraction,
fifth-percentile throughput, and coverage probability. Future-hotspot
prediction is left for a subsequent extension and is not assumed by
the present controller.
